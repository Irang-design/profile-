## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the polished clarity of modern engineering and design leadership. It blends high-precision technical discipline with understated sophistication, targeted at prospective clients, tech founders, design engineering leaders, and collaborators.

The visual style is **Contemporary Tech-Minimalism**:
- Pure, clean canvas spaces contrasted with ultra-fine structural borders.
- Crisp, intentional typographic hierarchy with tight tracking on headings for an authoritative, editorial cadence.
- Deliberate focal points powered by electric royal blue accents, keeping attention directed toward key achievements, project case studies, and points of contact.
- Quiet confidence conveyed through precise alignment, tactile micro-interactions, and balanced whitespace rather than decorative excess.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high legibility, strict contrast, and vivid focus accents.

- **Primary (`#1D4ED8`)**: Deep modern royal blue. Reserved strictly for key interactive elements, brand signatures, active navigation states, and primary call-to-actions.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep slate obsidian. Used for heavy display typography, primary headings, high-contrast dark accents, and anchor buttons.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8`)**: Electric sky blue. Used sparingly for interactive gradients, subtle hover glows, and live indicator pings.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate neutral. Drives secondary body text, micro-copy, inactive icons, and structured divider tones.
- **Surface Foundations**:
  - Background Base: `#FFFFFF` pure white canvas.
  - Surface Subdued: `#F8FAFC` for secondary card fills and section alternations.
  - Border Subtle: `#E2E8F0` for hairline segmentations.
  - Border Interactive: `#CBD5E1` for hover states and form outlines.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a three-tiered font pairing:
1. **Hanken Grotesk** powers all display and section headlines, delivering sharp, architectural authority with tightly kerned modern geometry.
2. **Inter** handles narrative copy and multi-line body paragraphs, optimizing screen legibility and effortless reading across both mobile and desktop screens.
3. **Geist** governs badges, metadata tags, numeric metrics, and interactive triggers, injecting a precise developer-grade technical personality into the interface.

## Layout & Spacing

The portfolio employs a fixed-width container layout centered within a fluid 12-column structural grid system:
- **Max Container Width**: 1200px max-width, maintaining comfortable ocular line-lengths for project briefs and bio narratives.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 1.25rem canvas margins and 1rem gutters. Stacks all project galleries, skill matrices, and contact forms into unified vertical sequences.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 1.5rem gutters, allowing 2-up project previews and split profile bios.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with 1.5rem gutters. Unlocks asymmetric balance—such as 5-column sticky profile overviews paired with 7-column case study streams.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy, artificial skeuomorphism in favor of **Tonal Layers with Low-Contrast Outlines and Ambient Diffusions**:

- **Hairline Outlines**: Surfaces are defined by 1px solid borders in `#E2E8F0`. This establishes crisp edges across all cards, badges, and modals on pure white backgrounds.
- **Ambient Floor Shadows**: Rest states on interactive project cards use ultra-diffused, cool-tinted shadows: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Active Elevation**: Upon hover or active focus, cards transition to `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.08)`, simultaneously stepping the border tone to `#CBD5E1` for a subtle lift effect.
- **Glass Utility**: Floating navigation headers use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over an 85% translucent white fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), edged by a bottom hairline border in `#E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework follows the **Rounded** baseline (`level 2`), producing modern, tech-forward ergonomics that soften technical precision without becoming whimsical:

- **Interactive Inputs & Buttons**: 0.5rem (`8px`) corner radius for standard targets.
- **Project & Case Study Cards**: 1rem (`16px`, `rounded-lg`) corner radius for content containers.
- **Modals & Hero Highlight Surfaces**: 1.5rem (`24px`, `rounded-xl`) corner radius.
- **Meta Badges & Pill Tags**: Fully rounded pill capsules (`9999px`) to visually segment compact taxonomy tags from structural rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#1D4ED8` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` radius, `label-md` Geist typography. Subtle translateY(-1px) and soft blue glow on hover.
- **Secondary**: Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Shifts to `#F8FAFC` background with `#CBD5E1` border on hover.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent background, `#1D4ED8` text with hover underline or icon slide micro-interaction.

### Badge Tags & Chips
- **Tech Stack Badges**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Rendered with `#F8FAFC` background, hairline `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, and `#0F172A` `label-sm` text.
- **Status Indicator Badge (e.g., "Available for Work")**: Soft tinted background (`#EFF6FF`), `#1D4ED8` text, featuring an animated 6px pulse dot in `#1D4ED8`.

### Project Cards
- Elevated card constructed with a `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and `1rem` corner radius.
- Includes a 16:10 aspect ratio media viewport with subtle inner border, project title in `headline-sm`, summary in `body-sm` (`#64748B`), and a horizontal flex row of Geist badge tags.
- Smooth transition (`200ms ease-out`) on hover: subtle 4px vertical rise, border shifts to `#CBD5E1`, and arrow action link transitions in color.

### Form Inputs & Contact Fields
- Text inputs and textareas feature a `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` radius, and `0.75rem 1rem` inner padding.
- Focused state: Hairline border transitions to `#1D4ED8` paired with a non-intrusive 3px ring in `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12)`.
- Labels styled in `label-md` using `#0F172A` with secondary assistive hints rendered in `#64748B`.

### Lists & Timelines (Experience & Education)
- Chronological timeline item connected by a 1px solid vertical track in `#E2E8F0`.
- Milestone points marked with a 10px circular node (`#FFFFFF` interior, 2px `#1D4ED8` border).
- Role title in `headline-sm`, organization name in `label-md` (`#1D4ED8`), and dates formatted cleanly in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### Floating Mobile Navigation
- Fixed bottom or top sticky container with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` surface.
- Minimal icons accompanied by `label-sm` active state labels, highlighted via `#1D4ED8` accents.
